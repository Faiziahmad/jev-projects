Questions

Good to know.

Something else on your mind? Just ask me.

Will testing break my website?+

No. I test carefully and only what we agreed on. Anything that could affect your live site, I check with you first, or we test on a copy instead.

How long does it take?+

It depends on how big your setup is. I'll give you a fixed timeline before we start, so you know exactly when to expect the report.

What do I get at the end?+

A short summary for managers, a detailed section for your developers with steps to fix each problem, a call to go through it together, and a free re-check once you've made the fixes.

Do you work with small businesses?+

Yes. Whether it's one website or a whole company network, I'll scope the work to fit your size and budget.

Is my information kept private?+

Always. I'm happy to sign a privacy agreement before we talk details, and everything I find is shared only with you.

Worried about something?

Tell me what you'd like checked. I'll reply within a day with honest advice, even if you don't end up hiring me.
